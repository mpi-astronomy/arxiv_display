|**Abstract**|            We present a systematic interferometric search for close supermassive black hole (SMBH) binaries in 20 nearby type 1 active galactic nuclei (AGN) observed with VLTI/\grav. |**Abstract**|            Direct imaging and characterization of rocky exoplanets remains one of the central challenges in exoplanetary science. |**Abstract**|            The mechanism driving the evolution and dispersal of protoplanetary discs has a substantial impact on the theories of planet formation.
